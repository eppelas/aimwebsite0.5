# Current Site Readable Baseline

Control prototype: close to current aimindset.org structure and content, rebuilt after review as a real homepage surface instead of a structural/meta page.

## Sources

- https://aimindset.org/ — primary text/link source.
- https://health.aimindset.org/ and https://selfdev.aimindset.org/ — visual rhythm references only.
- Local V3 assets under `public/assets/` — case and people imagery.

## Borrowed Formally

- Current site order, product ecosystem, stats, and proof rhythm.
- Improved line length and body readability without changing the source message.

## Not Copied

- No external layout, brand identity, typeface, or asset was copied.
- No new product claims, fake testimonials, fake reviews, or archive links were invented.

## 2026-05-28 Feedback Response

- Avoids the rejected beige/yellow AIM OS grid, monospaced Russian body copy, and system-card wall.
- Uses proportional body typography and keeps visual effects outside the main reading surface.
- Keeps current aimindset.org content density as the baseline.
- Removes meta QA cards such as "where are reviews" / "main CTA leads..." and replaces them with site-facing product, format, work, people, and join sections.
- Adds a neutral light visual stage with local AIM imagery, participant work thumbnails, and a pointer-reactive signal field.

## 2026-05-29 Product / Footer Fix

- Reworked the ecosystem/product block after review: removed the empty pale lab slab, added a dark interactive orbit canvas inside the main lab card, and kept the existing source-backed product copy and links.
- Reworked `join` and footer from a horizontal tag list into a real CTA surface plus grouped navigation/legal links.
- This is still a review-only prototype; the interaction and composition need human rating before any reuse.
